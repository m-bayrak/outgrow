# The soul becomes the comment pointer

**Idea.** Explore an alternative continuation of version 04 in which the protagonist's flight itself becomes the comment pointer, rather than entering the contour bundle and resolving as typing dots. This was an earlier Codex alternative, not a request to replace the approved 04 sequence.

**Details.**
- Keep the black/white/orange palette and low contour wings.
- The seed turns down and right; its thin trail bends around “Meaning / in the / comments.” and its leading shape stretches toward the comment area.
- The [concept still](../references/images/comments-soul-pointer-study.png) shows a literal arrowhead. The owner later preferred no arrowhead; if revisited, test a rounded directional seed or open trail first.
- A brief anticipation, glide and one soft directional nudge would guide attention without a persistent bounce. The suggested 2.5–3 second beat was exploratory, not an approved duration.
- Builds on 04's narrative and typography; it would change the final motion grammar and requires a new version.
