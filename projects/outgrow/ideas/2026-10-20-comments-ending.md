# Comments ending: send the viewer to the comments

**Idea.** After "Break the shape. Keep becoming.", the film moves on to a last frame that makes the viewer open the comments, where the idea, story and intent of the video are written out. The soul does not just point at the comments: it becomes the lines that lead there.

**Target frame.** `references/images/meaning-in-the-comments-target.png` (941 × 1672, made with image generation): "Meaning / in the / comments." with "comments." orange, white contour lines from the lower left converging on an orange comment bubble at the right, wings at the bottom. The owner wants these changes against it:
- The comment icon moves **18 px down and 22 px left** in the target's own pixels (≈ 21 down and 25 left at 1080 × 1920; confirm the unit with the owner).
- The soul and its long trace are **not** placed as in the target: they pull attention away from the comment icon, which must be the focus.
- The text sits **lower**, closer to the guiding lines.

**Where the real button is.** The owner overlaid an iPhone and an Android screenshot of the YouTube app (Shorts) and marked the comment button area: in a 9:16 frame it sits in the right-hand action column at roughly 60 % of the height. The screenshots are third-party UI and are not stored; measure against the target frame and the description above. Keep text out of the right action column and the bottom ~20 % (channel name and title overlay).

## Owner's sequence (verbatim intent, translated)

1. Do not place the soul and its tail as in the target; the main attention goes to the comment icon.
2. The text is lower, closer to the lines that point to the comment.
3. Make the previous text disappear somehow.
4. The soul flies, a tail may follow it and stay for a while, then fade (or another effect). It flies again and wherever it lands, text appears, reusing the effects from the previous ending.
5. When the text is done, the soul flies to the lower-left end of the lines and touches them; that part of the lines turns orange as if the soul became part of them. The lines then stretch toward the comment icon (their leading ends orange) and at the point above they arrive and draw the comment icon.
6. Inside the icon the soul flickers, bursts or moves.

## Claude's proposals (not yet confirmed)

- **Exit of the old text:** the soul "inhales" the letters of "Break the shape. Keep becoming." one by one; it grows a little with each, carrying the meaning on. Simpler fallback: the spark runs back along the lines and erases them.
- **New text:** "Meaning in the" written by a glide, "comments." in orange by an impact, the same grammar as version 03.
- **Lines to the icon:** the soul touches the lower-left ends, orange travels along the lines (the existing travelling-colour effect), the line tips stretch and converge on one point, and the bubble outline is drawn from it like a pen stroke.
- **Inside the icon:** the soul splits into three dots pulsing in turn (a "typing…" indicator), then they settle into the two text bars of the bubble; the bubble pulses gently.
- **End:** ~2 s of calm pulse with thin rings travelling toward the real button, so there is time to tap and a replay starts softly.
- **Placement caveat:** our bubble sits on the video under YouTube's real button, whose position differs by phone. Place ours slightly left of the real one so it points to it rather than duplicating it with a visible offset; the converge point uses the owner's offset.
- **Length:** about +6–7 s, ~23 s in total.
