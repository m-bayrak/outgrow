# Five directions for the ending keyframe

**Idea.** Continue from Claude's four-petal wing finale with the headline “Break the shape. Keep becoming.” Five image-generated concepts explore how typography can inhabit the existing contours.

**Details.**
- [Beyond the frame](../references/images/ending-01-beyond-frame.png): enlarged asymmetric wings extend beyond the edges; text occupies black negative space.
- [Between the wings](../references/images/ending-02-between-wings.png): a symmetric central passage frames the headline.
- [Under pressure](../references/images/ending-03-under-pressure.png): an elastic contour shell tightly surrounds the words; the soul pushes outward.
- [Trace of freedom](../references/images/ending-04-trace-of-freedom.png): the wings sit low, a fine orange flight trace rises into open black space, and the headline sits left-aligned. This direction was selected for refinement.
- [Words as shell](../references/images/ending-05-words-as-shell.png): monumental text becomes the structure the soul passes through.
- These are concept stills, not rendered frames from the code. Generated font appearance approximates the video reference; production uses Inter Tight 800 from STYLE.md.

**Next.** Keep the four alternatives as explorations. Continue the selected trace-of-freedom direction.
