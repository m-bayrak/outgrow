# Text ending: the trace of freedom

**Idea.** After the wings form, the figure drops to the bottom of the frame, the soul flies an S-curve upward and freezes. A second spark leaves it, and wherever it lands or jumps, words appear.

**Target frame.** `references/images/trace-of-freedom-orange-verbs.png`.

**How it was built.**
- The camera, not the figure, moves: it rises with the soul, so the wings sink. The orange trace is the soul's own flight path.
- Verbs (**Break**, **Keep**) burst out of the spark's landing point with an orange shock ring.
- "the shape." and "becoming." are written letter by letter as the spark glides along the baseline; fresh letters glow orange and cool to white.
- The spark ends as the full stop of "becoming.".
